[b]All the Progression goodness, less research.[/b]

[i]This is a fork of [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3634874325]Progression: Attire[/url] by ferny and LemonSkin. Huge thanks to ferny and LemonSkin for the original mod and all the Progression work this builds on. For a really good modpack, check out [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3521297585]The Progression Modpack[/url].[/i]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Reworks clothing research, so apparel from the base game and many mods unlocks in its proper tech era.
[*]Adds the tailor bench, plus vanity and costuming tables that gather fine clothes, costumes and masks from many mods.
[*]De-duplicates attire from various mods and sorts it into tidy categories by era.
[/list]

[h2]Why "LESS"?[/h2]
Progression: Attire adds up to [b]18 research projects[/b] depending on your mods. This fork groups them into [b]at most 9[/b]:
[list]
[*][b]Leafwear[/b], [b]Heavy tribalwear[/b] and [b]War tribalwear[/b] (Animal, on the VFE Tribals tab).
[*][b]Tribal accessories[/b] (Neolithic).
[*][b]Tailor bench[/b], [b]Vanity and costuming tables[/b] and [b]Leather armor[/b] (Medieval).
[*][b]Electric tailor bench[/b] and [b]Gas mask[/b] (Industrial).
[/list]
Everything else unlocks with the research it already builds on:
[list]
[*]River's Tribal Shoes: [b]Tribalwear[/b].
[/list]
Everything stays in the same era, and still needs the other research it did before. The tailoring research on the main tab is laid out with no overlaps.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Keeps all of the original's mod support, including:
[list]
[*]Royalty, Ideology, Biotech, Anomaly and Odyssey.
[*]Medieval Overhaul, VFE Medieval 2, VFE Classical, VFE Tribals, VFE Empire, VFE Pirates and VFE Settlers.
[*]Vanilla Apparel, Armor, Psycasts, Genetics, Gravship and Anomaly Expanded, Hats and Rags, VFE Art and VRE Fungoid.
[*]VQE Generator, Cryptoforge, Ancients and Deadlife.
[*]Det's Xenotypes, Spacer Arsenal, Alpha Genes, Altered Carbon, Impact Weaponry, Tacticool Ops and Samurai Faction.
[*]Kid Apparel Expanded, Toddlers, River's Tribal Shoes and Mittens, Leaf Skirt, Bandage Wraps, and Roo's Minotaur and Satyr/Faun.
[*]Victorian Era Apparels, Ponpeco Accessories and Pajamas, Unagi, Cinderella, Imperial Attires, Oracle's Miscellania and other apparel mods, sorted onto the vanity and costuming tables.
[*]Both Progression: Furniture and ProgressionLESS: Furniture, Better Architect and Node Research.
[/list]

[b]Replaces the original. Don't use both.[/b]

[b]Switching mid-game isn't recommended.[/b] Many of the original's research projects are merged or removed, so swapping Progression: Attire for this fork in an existing save can lose research progress. Start a new game when you switch.
